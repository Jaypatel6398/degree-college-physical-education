B.P.Ed. (Bachelor of Physical Education)

Eligibility Criteria for B.P.Ed. (Bachelor of Physical Education)

  1. Pass with 50% aggregate marks in Bachelor's degree (any discipline) and having at least participation in the Inter College or Inter Zonal or District or School competition in sports and games as recognized by the AIU or IOA or SGFI or Govt. of India or equivalent.
  2. OR
  3. Pass with 45% aggregate marks in Bachelor's degree in Physical Education or equivalent.
  4. OR
  5. Pass with 45% aggregate marks in Bachelor's degree (any discipline) with Physical Education as compulsory or elective subject or equivalent.
  6. OR
  7. Pass with 45% aggregate marks in Bachelor's degree and participated in National or Inter University or State competitions or secured 1st, 2nd or 3rd position in Inter College or Inter-Zonal or District or School competition in sports and games as recognized by the AIU or IOA or SGFI or Govt. of India or equivalent.
  8. OR
  9. Pass in Bachelor's degree with participation in International Competitions or secured 1st, 2nd or 3rd position in National or Inter-University competition in sports and games as recognized by respective federations or AIU or IOA or SGFI or Govt. of India or equivalent.
  10. OR
  11. Pass with 45% aggregate marks in Graduation and at least three years of teaching experience for deputed in-service candidates (i.e. trained physical education teachers or coaches).

Note: Medical Fitness Certificate (as per university prescribed format) is Compulsory.

5% relaxation in aggregate marks for SC or ST or OBC candidates. [Any one of the relaxations shall be applicable to candidates having achievements in sports].

Admission Criteria

Merit prepared with weightage to qualifying exam and sports achievement, subject to eligibility criteria.
